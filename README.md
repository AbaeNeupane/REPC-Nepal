## About

**Rights, Equity and Peace Campaign – Nepal (REPC-Nepal)** is a non-profit, non-governmental organization registered under the *Association Registration Act, 2034*, and headquartered in Thapathali, Kathmandu.

Founded in **2083 B.S.**, the organization was established on the belief that every citizen deserves to live with dignity, access justice without barriers, and exist within a society built on equity and lasting peace. REPC-Nepal works primarily for those most often left behind — women, children, the elderly, persons with disabilities, and economically marginalized communities — through rights advocacy, free legal aid, mediation, and peace-building.

---

## Mission

To advocate for human rights, provide free legal services to vulnerable groups, promote mediation as a meaningful alternative to litigation, and build a culture of sustainable peace — from the community level to the national level.

---

## Vision

A Nepal where every citizen enjoys their fundamental rights with dignity; where disputes are resolved through dialogue, tolerance, and reconciliation; and where peace is not an aspiration but a lived reality at every level of society.

## What We Do

**Free Legal Aid** — Legal counselling and representation provided at no cost to disadvantaged individuals, single women, children, elderly persons, and persons with disabilities.

**Mediation & Conflict Resolution** — Training certified mediators and facilitating peaceful dispute resolution at the local and judicial level, reducing the burden on courts and restoring community harmony.

**Training & Capacity Building** — Specialized programs covering human rights, mediation, child rights, juvenile justice, cyber security, and good governance — delivered to individuals, organizations, and government bodies.

**Research & Documentation** — Studies, reports, and legal documentation on human rights conditions, climate change impacts, child justice, and mediation practice in Nepal.

**Rights Advocacy** — Campaigns advocating for the rights of women, children, the elderly, and persons with disabilities — challenging discrimination, exploitation, and exclusion at every level.

**Peace Building** — Promoting a nationwide culture of dialogue, tolerance, and co-existence, institutionalizing reconciliation as a foundation for a stable Nepal.

**Climate & Human Rights** — Studying the intersection of climate change and human rights, with a focus on communities most vulnerable to its impacts.

## Affiliations

REPC-Nepal is affiliated with the **Social Welfare Council** (Samaj Kalyan Parishad) under the Social Welfare Act, 2049, and the **Mediation Council of Nepal** (Melmilaap Parishad) under the Mediation Act, 2068.
